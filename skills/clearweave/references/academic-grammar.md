# Academic grammar and rhetorical choices

Use for English papers, Chinese academic text, and explicitly requested academic translation. Edit the grammatical relation, not the perceived sophistication of the vocabulary. The user’s venue guidance and established spelling convention settle genuine style variants.

<a id="cw39"></a>
## CW39 · Tense and aspect by communicative function

| Function | Common choice | Distinction to preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Established fact, definition, or algorithm behavior | Present | An algorithm description can remain present inside Methods. |
| Completed experiment or data collection | Past | “We tested” reports work already performed. |
| A body of work with current relevance | Present perfect or present | Use past when referring to one dated study’s actions. |
| Table/figure content | Present | “Table 2 reports” describes the document. |
| Interpretation | Present with calibrated modality | Separate interpretation from the completed observation. |
| Proposed evaluation | Future or explicitly planned | Never turn a proposal into a completed result. |

Avoid a blanket “Methods must be past” rule. Keep local time references coherent. Chinese aspect words (已、正在、拟、将、尚未) carry the corresponding distinctions; do not remove them as clutter.

<a id="cw40"></a>
## CW40 · Agreement, articles, and word forms

Find the grammatical head, including through citations and intervening phrases. A coordinated pair normally takes plural agreement; an established unit name may be singular. Match relative-clause verbs to the actual antecedent. Correct articles according to meaning and pronunciation, countability, number, and whether an entity has been introduced. Do not convert all “data” or collective-noun agreement to one dialect.

Check singular/plural forms such as criterion/criteria, analysis/analyses, and phenomenon/phenomena. Distinguish affect/effect and imply/infer by actual meaning. Treat statistical significance, efficiency, effectiveness, and robustness as different concepts. Keep user-defined technical terms stable.

In exact quotations, record a grammar issue outside the quote. In editable prose, correct it. Citation keys, mathematics, code, URLs, and protected regions retain their content.

<a id="cw41"></a>
## CW41 · Information flow and sentence architecture

Link familiar information to the next sentence’s point; place the important new information where it is easy to find. Bring a distant subject and verb together when this reduces ambiguity. Maintain parallel grammar in actual lists, comparisons, and contribution statements. Keep necessary long sentences when they express one coherent relation.

Resolve pronouns only when the antecedent is supported. Check dangling modifiers: the entity that performs an introductory action must match the subject. Inspect restrictive versus supplementary relative clauses for changes to the set being described. Restrictive “which” can be valid British usage; do not perform a universal which-to-that replacement.

中文先确认主语与修饰对象，再拆长定语、补足原稿已明确的动作主体、调整信息顺序。必要的“但是、因此、尤其、仅在”表达逻辑关系，保留其作用；不要为节奏强行切成短句。

<a id="cw42"></a>
## CW42 · Grammar that changes the claim

Check the placement and scope of only, all, not, respectively, at least, approximately, and their Chinese equivalents. Preserve whether a difference is absolute or relative and whether a comparison concerns accuracy, cost, latency, or another metric.

Prediction is not causation: “predicts a label” can describe a machine-learning task without a causal claim. “Significant” may mean a reported statistical result or informal emphasis; inspect evidence before editing. An untested mechanism remains a hypothesis. Grammar correction never supplies missing results or causal identification.

## Section-function map

| Section | Reader’s question | Writing moves |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Title / abstract | What was done and what was found? | Specific subject, task/gap, method, result, conditions. |
| Introduction | Why this problem and this contribution? | Relevant context, precise gap, response, supported contribution. |
| Related work | How does this fit existing approaches? | Group by mechanism or question; fairly characterize cited work. |
| Methods | How does it work; how can it be reproduced? | Objects and notation before dependencies; decisions, procedures, settings. |
| Results | What did the evaluation show? | Question, comparison, metric, finding, material trade-off. |
| Discussion | What does the finding support? | Interpretation, alternative explanation, applicability, remaining uncertainty. |
| Conclusion | What should the reader retain? | Established takeaway and its significance. |
| Reviewer response | What concern was addressed? | Answer, evidence, change/location, remaining substantive issue. |

These are selectable moves, not a mandatory sentence count or a promise that every paper has a theorem. Keep the existing section organization when it already serves the task. Diagnosis may return a reverse outline; ordinary polishing returns the edited prose.
