# Compare academic editing guidance

Use when the user requests several writing skills, journal or supervisor guidance,
or a comparison of editing approaches. Extend the existing preservation and
sample-profile modules; a routine paragraph edit does not need this workflow.

## Establish which guidance applies

Keep a compact working record of the article type, target journal and section,
editing scope, authoritative draft and protected claims. Separate these inputs:

| Input | How it can guide this draft |
| --- | --- |
| Current journal instructions | Verify the applicable article type, version and exact requirement; an example article is not the instruction. |
| Actual supervisor or author feedback | Preserve the requested purpose and scope; do not invent feedback or attribute general advice to the user's professor. |
| Professor or institutional teaching material | Apply the relevant reasoning principle as advice; record what was actually read. |
| Published samples | Select by genre, section and comparison task. Learn argument moves rather than distinctive wording or source facts. |
| Editing skills and blogs | Generate candidate improvements; their popularity and agreement do not establish correctness. |

Within the authorized edit, preserve research meaning and evidence before applying
style. Reconcile the user's instructions with actual venue requirements. When they
conflict materially, identify the exact conflict and continue unaffected work.
Do not replace this judgment with a universal ranking of all professors or skills.

A review's way of synthesizing comparable studies may help a research article's
related work. Its search-method chapter, taxonomy or reporting checklist does not
automatically belong in that experimental article. Apply the same genre check to
legal, product, clinical and conversational writing guidance.

## Give each proposal a reason to exist

When comparison is requested, record only enough to adjudicate the actual edits:

```text
Skill/source and version; exact draft location
Original -> proposed wording
Paragraph function and concrete problem
Evidence/definition the edit depends on
Meaning risk and any conflicting guidance
Decision: adopt / adapt / keep original / defer
```

No proposed change is a valid result. Do not require one edit per skill, force a
winner or keep applying different skills until every paragraph has changed.
Deduplicate equivalent suggestions and evaluate them once. Several skills may
share an ancestor; their votes are not independent peer review.

Compare the original and candidate on three separate questions: does it preserve
the research proposition, does it make a specific reading task easier, and does
it fit this venue/genre? Preserve a clear original when the benefit is only cosmetic
or the intended referent remains unresolved. Record a reason rather than an
unmeasured percentage or arbitrary quality score.

When the user requests scores, define an anchored ordinal rubric before applying
it. Score only the named manuscript/excerpt and inspected advice: for example,
meaning preservation, issue specificity and added usefulness. Give a location and
reason for each rating; use NA for unread or unavailable evidence. Keep the lens's
assessment separate from the integrating editor's decision. Do not average unlike
dimensions into an acceptance, AI-authorship or universal skill-quality percentage.

## Check objects and scope, not just words

Before simplifying a noun phrase, identify its level: dataset, training cohort,
individual request, reference set, prediction set, metric or aggregate. A symbol
must still denote the object defined in Methods. Matching the numerals is not
enough if a value moves from a threshold to a result or a request count becomes an
independent-study count.

After splitting, merging or moving a clause, check what its condition, negation,
exception and final modifier govern. Keep a condition with the result it restricts;
do not let a sentence break turn a conditional finding into a general conclusion.
Keep a readable long sentence when a proposed split creates ambiguity.

For anti-defensive edits, test the sentence's argument function. An actual failure,
counterexample, uncertainty, source limit or necessary distinction remains visible.
An imagined objection or internal editing narrative can be removed when it adds no
information the reader needs. A direct statement may still require a substantive
negative clause. There is no caveat, connector, passive-voice or sentence-length quota.

## Apply once and verify the combined draft

Apply the selected minimal edits to the authoritative draft, then reread the edited
passages in context. Two individually faithful edits can produce a new referent,
duplicate argument or misplaced condition when combined. Recheck the affected
tables, captions, abstract, conclusion, supplement and exports as appropriate.
Stop after resolving the identified defects; another stylistic pass is justified by
a new issue, not by the number of tools available.

For reusable skill maintenance, use synthetic counterexamples outside the private
manuscript. Separate source/structure checks, authored fixtures and actual model
trials. This module does not measure authorship, detector success or journal acceptance.

## Source notes

This procedure is original guidance combining observed editing failures with the
existing ClearWeave preservation and voice contracts. Useful external principles:

- [MIT Communication Lab: Introduction](https://mitcommlab.mit.edu/be/commkit/journal-article-introduction/) and [Discussion](https://mitcommlab.mit.edu/eecs/commkit/journal-article-discussion/): connect the reader's question, evidence and interpretation; adapt to audience and venue.
- [K-Dense scientific-writing](https://github.com/K-Dense-AI/scientific-agent-skills/tree/5f2bc0341354fd5590aef76a533ada1e1e5b4e62/skills/scientific-writing): retain the concepts and analysis roles attached to quantities.
- [Deslop](https://github.com/fayerman-source/deslop/tree/0d5860229f7623a05b2c9544c41c3d18b8b9cfe1): preserve condition and modifier scope when restructuring prose.

These are attributed sources of ideas, not copied instructions, examples, figures
or code. Their publication, licensing and policy claims must be assessed separately;
their rules are not inherited wholesale or turned into journal requirements.
